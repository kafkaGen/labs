# PRD: <project or feature name>

**Status:** Draft / Building / Shipped
**One-line pitch:** <what this is, in one sentence>

## 1. Problem (always)

- What's broken or missing today?
- What do people do about it today (workarounds, existing tools, nothing)?
- Why should this exist at all? What happens if nobody builds it?
- Why are you (or this team) well placed to build it?
- Why now?

## 2. Target users (always)

- Who is this for: customers, other companies, or internal users?
- Roughly how many of them are there, and how would they find out this exists?
- If there's more than one kind of user, which is the key one?
- Walk through one or two scenarios: how does this person actually use it, start to finish?

## 3. Definition of done (always)

- What's the one measurable or checkable outcome that tells you this worked?
- How exactly will you measure it, and what number or signal counts as a pass?

## 4. Scope (always)

**In for v1:**
- ...

**Out of scope for now (and why):**
- ...

## 5. Features

Prioritized must-have / nice-to-have. For each: which problem from Section 1 does it address, and what's the smallest version that still solves it? State it as the problem it solves, not the implementation.

- [must] ...
- [nice] ...

## 6. Minimum acceptable criteria (skip for hobby or internal tools)

- Performance: how fast/responsive does this need to be?
- Reliability: what's the tolerable failure rate or downtime?
- Security: what data or access needs protecting, and from whom?
- Support: how will issues get reported, and by when do they get fixed?

## 7. Assumptions & risks (skip if there genuinely are none)

- About users: ...
- About the technology: ...
- About the business model: ...
- What could kill this, and is there a cheap way to check before it does?

## 8. Open questions (skip once nothing is left unresolved)

- Unresolved item, and who or what resolves it (a person, a background research subagent, a test).

## 9. Timeline (skip unless there's a real external deadline)

- Target date, and the reason for that specific date.
